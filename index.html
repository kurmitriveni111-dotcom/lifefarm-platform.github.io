MEMBERSHIP

One permanent Member Key

Generated after agreement and all required details. The member is signed in automatically and receives a downloadable identity card.

Reference codes

Unique codes are allocated from a 10,000-code registry. Each member can keep up to three recent codes; issued codes are never reused in this browser registry.

Saved member record

Your username and member details are saved in this browser. The Member Key is stored in encrypted form and as a verification hash. The password is stored as a salted PBKDF2 verification hash, never as readable text.

Member rank ladder

Every new member starts as New Member. The ladder is New Member · Active Member · Contributor · Senior Contributor · Specialist · Team Captain · Operations Officer · Administrator.

Private access control

Each member can access only their own identity and reference-code controls.
