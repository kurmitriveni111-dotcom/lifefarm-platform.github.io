2. Privacy Notice

This edition is a static website. It does not provide server-side member registration, member login, private cloud storage, or a central member database.

2.1 Browser storage

Some optional utilities, such as a locally generated participation card, language preference and policy acknowledgement, may be stored in your browser's local storage. This information remains on the device/browser profile unless you clear it, export it or share it yourself. It is not automatically synchronized with LIFEFARM.

2.2 Hosting and technical logs

The hosting provider may process technical request data, such as IP address, browser information, request time and security logs, under its own service terms and privacy documentation. The website operator should review the selected provider's terms and configure any analytics or logging before use.

2.3 Contact email

If you email LIFEFARM, the information you include is processed by the email service and the people handling the enquiry. Do not email passwords, financial credentials, Member Keys, government identity documents or other unnecessary sensitive information.

2.4 Cookies and analytics

This static edition does not intentionally set advertising cookies or run third-party analytics by default. Hosting, browser or optional embedded services may have their own technical processing. If analytics or additional services are added, this notice must be updated before activation.

2.5 Your choices

You can clear browser site data to remove locally saved card/preferences. You can avoid entering optional details. For a privacy question, contact lifefarm7531@gmail.com.
